Why cache-off cycles rose although the wait states fell from 7 to 3

The full account, with file:line, RM0468 quotes and the arithmetic, is in ~/vl-v3-proof/h723-cache-off-cycles.md. In short:

The clocks: - Version 3 (firmware/common/stm32h723/board.h at 4032f80, lines 120–125) runs the core at 520 MHz with HPRE /2: rcc_hclk3 = rcc_aclk = 260 MHz, the flash's AXI clock. LATENCY is 3. - Version 2 ran core and AXI at 64 MHz, with the power-on latency of 7.

What the manual says: RM0468 §4.3.8 and Table 16 count the latency in cycles of the flash's AXI interface clock.

The arithmetic: - An array read costs (7 + 1) × 1 = 8 core cycles before and (3 + 1) × 2 = 8 now: the same. - Everything else an access pays on the AXI side is counted in AXI cycles, and an AXI cycle is now 2 core cycles.

Measured (H0, etl crc32_t256, cache off, per byte from 256 to 1024 bytes): - 32 → 38 cycles at placement 0, 34 → 42 at placement 4. The placement's extra access costs 2 core cycles before and 4 now: the predicted doubling, seen directly. - With the cache on, 8 = 8.

Time: 1024 bytes, cache off, is 32 796 cycles at 64 MHz (512 µs) before and 38 968 cycles at 520 MHz (74.9 µs) now. Cycles at another clock are not times.

Not explained: - the number of AXI accesses per call (the M7's fetch with the I-cache off, the flash's read queue); - why a configuration's per-call rise (+28 at length 1) exceeds the baseline's (+23).

An access-counting test (the loop from ITCM, or the table in DTCM, at both clocks) would settle both. None was run.

The watched power-on of the nRF5340 DK: passed

The owner decided on one watched power-on, after the reruns. It ran after pools as well, with the reviewer's OK (15:38 UTC). The question was whether the debug port is open after a power-on with only our boot stub having run. That is what BENCHBOOT_NRF5340 is for: it copies the UICR to the CTRL-AP at reset. All of it was zero write.

How the switch was made. - The queue's rule "never power the nRF5340" stays. - It was lifted for this one call by an explicit flag, boardqueue.py power nrf5340dk off|on --override-no-power REASON (4c927bb). The flag runs the switch under the hub's lock and writes the call to ~/vl-queue/power-overrides.tsv before and after. - Hub 4B0708619185 had no job. All of today's records were pushed.
