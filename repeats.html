Repeated by others

Every number on these pages was measured in one lab, on one board of each model. This page lists every time someone outside the project ran our tests on their own board and sent us the result, whether it agreed with ours or not.

Take part

You need one of the six board models of the comparison, a Linux machine and the USB cable of the board's own debug probe. Then, in a clone of the repository:

./repeat.sh --check     # tools, toolchain image and board
./repeat.sh             # 15-25 minutes; --level full: 2-4 hours

repeat.sh builds the firmware we ran, byte for byte, runs it on your board, compares your results with ours and packs everything into one archive. Mail the archive to contact@verified-libs.org. The details are on Reproduce.

The quick level writes the board's flash three times (one benchmark bundle, two stress tests); the full level once per stress mode and once per benchmark bundle of the board (2 to 18, listed on Reproduce). Nothing else on the board is changed.

What happens with your result

  • We check that the firmware is the one we ran (its checksums) and compare your numbers and stress verdicts with ours, the same way repeat.sh did.
  • With your consent, it is listed below, under the name or handle you choose, or anonymously, and the archive goes into the repository.
  • A result that disagrees with ours is looked into before anything else. It is listed as it is, together with what we found. If the error is ours, the page is corrected and says so.

There is no payment. What we can offer is the credit here, and a test setup you can reuse for your own buffer code.
