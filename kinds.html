Results

Three kinds of library are tested so far. Each has its own results, method and records; the numbers of one kind are not compared with another's. The kinds are in the order they were added.

Embedded ring buffers

  • What is measured: cycles per push and pop and per block, code size, an interrupt stress test in which a deliberately broken buffer must be caught, and model checks where a library promises lock-free concurrency.
  • Libraries: 29 (counted by repository), in 38 configurations.
  • Boards: 6.
  • Results: results page
  • Method: method page
  • Comparison page: Compare
  • Records: records/
  • How to repeat: Reproduce

CRC calculation

  • What is measured: cycles of one call that computes a CRC, for messages of 1 to 1024 bytes, with the cache off and on, and the bytes of flash and RAM it takes; by libraries that compute the CRC in software and, on the boards that have one, by the board's CRC unit as a row beside them. Every call's result is checked against a reference.
  • Libraries: 8, in 35 configurations, for 3 CRCs.
  • The board's CRC unit: on 4 boards, driven by our code (not a library), a row beside the libraries.
  • Boards: 6.
  • Results: results page
  • Method: method page
  • Comparison page: Compare
  • Records: records/crc
  • How to repeat: Reproduce, "The CRC records"

Fixed-block memory pools

  • What is measured: cycles of alloc and free of one block size (32 blocks of 32 bytes) in six scenarios and of creating the pool, with the cache off and on, and the bytes of flash and RAM it takes; every call is checked on the board. Measured in one context. Use from interrupts is tested in a series of its own, the concurrency series: an interrupt at every cycle of a call, with controls that must be caught.
  • Libraries: 10, in 10 configurations, and a row of ours (not a library).
  • Boards: 6.
  • Results: results page
  • Method: method page
  • Comparison page: Compare
  • Records: records/pools
  • How to repeat: Reproduce, "The pools records"
