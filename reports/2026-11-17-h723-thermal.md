# NUCLEO-H723ZG at 520 MHz: die temperature under full load (2026-11-16/17)

The owner asked how warm the board gets in its closed cabinet at 520 MHz
(the fast-clock option, `common/stm32h723/board.h`, BOARD_CLOCK_FAST).
`firmware/h7-thermal` logs the die temperature from the chip's digital
temperature sensor (factory calibration T0 30 C, FMT0 711.0 kHz, ramp
2035 Hz/C) while the core runs FPU and integer work on DTCM data at 520 MHz,
then cools at 64 MHz. Stop at 85 C (never reached); VOS0 allows a junction
temperature of 105 C (DS13313 Rev 5).

| Run | Idle 64 MHz | Load 520 MHz | Peak | End of load | Cooled to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| short (1/10/5 min, 1 s samples) | 31 C | 10 min | 50.0 C | 49.7 C | 34.2 C after 5 min |
| long (5/60/45 min, 2 s samples) | 32.2 C | 60 min | 52.3 C | 51.5 C | 31.1 C after 45 min |

Long run during the load: +1 min 44.1 C, +5 min 48.0, +10 min 49.7, +20 min
51.2, +30 min 51.2, +40 min 51.5, +60 min 51.7.

**Time constants** (sum of three exponentials fitted to the long run, rms
0.18-0.19 C, about the sensor's step; grid fit, so the values are rough):

| | Load | Cooling | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| step at the switch | < 2 s | < 2 s | the die (faster than the samples) |
| fast | 6 s, +5.4 C | 8 s, -4.9 C | die and package |
| middle | 300 s, +6.9 C | 300 s, -6.2 C | the board's copper |
| slow | ~900 s, +1.5 C | ~900 s, -2.3 C | board as a whole, cabinet air |

- The rise levels off: after 20 minutes it adds about 0.5 C in the next 40.
  The cabinet does not keep warming the chip in a way these 60 minutes show.
- After 45 minutes of cooling the die is at 31.1 C, below the 32.2 C it
  started at (which still had the short run's heat in it): nothing builds up.
- Total rise about 20 C for about 0.45 W (DS13313 table 20: ~135 mA at
  3.3 V) is about 44 C/W; the datasheet gives 44.8 C/W for LQFP144.
- The benchmark at 520 MHz runs for about a minute and then returns to
  64 MHz (board_idle), so in use the die sees the fast step, not the
  50 C plateau.
