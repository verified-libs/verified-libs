# Contributing to verified-libs

> **Draft (2026-11-15).** This agreement is not yet in use and has not been
> reviewed by a lawyer. Outside contributions are accepted only after it is.

Suggestions of libraries and reports of mistakes are welcome by mail
(contact@verified-libs.org) without any agreement: see
[README](README.md), "Suggest a library".

Code, text or data that you want added to this repository (a patch, a pull
request, a record) is accepted only under the agreement below, so that the
project's work stays in one hand and can later be moved to a verified-libs
association or company as a whole.

## How to agree

Add this line to the description of every commit you contribute:

```
Agreed-to: CONTRIBUTING.md v1
```

With it you confirm that you have read the agreement below (version 1) and
accept it for that contribution. Without it, a contribution is not merged.

Each person who contributes material to a pull request accepts the agreement
for their own contribution, with this line in their own commits. Nobody can
accept for someone else. A later version of the agreement does not change the
terms for contributions accepted under an earlier version.

## In short

- What is merged of your contribution, you hand over to the project. What
  is not merged, the project gets no rights in.
- You may keep using your own contribution for anything.
- The project publishes its code under MIT and its texts and results under
  CC BY 4.0.
- The rights can move on only to a verified-libs entity, or to a non-profit
  organisation that takes over the project, and only if it takes over the
  obligations towards you.
- You are named in AUTHORS unless you ask not to be.

This summary is a help for reading. The agreement text below is what counts.
